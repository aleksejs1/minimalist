## Where Does Clutter Go?

A major trap of hasty decluttering is treating the landfill as an out-of-sight garbage dump. Tossing bags of plastic items into the trash merely relocates clutter from your private home to our shared planet.

Sustainable minimalism unites intentional simplicity with ecological stewardship.

> "The most sustainable product is the one you already own."

## The 5R Hierarchy of Mindful Consumption

1. **Refuse:** Say no to promotional swag, single-use bags, flyers, and cheap freebies.
2. **Reduce:** Decrease overall volume of new purchases by asking the 5 core questions.
3. **Reuse / Repair:** Mend damaged clothing, repair electronics, choose durable reusable containers.
4. **Rot:** Compost organic food scraps and natural materials.
5. **Recycle:** The last resort for materials (glass, aluminum, paper, and clean plastics).

## Responsible Item Offloading in Europe & Baltics
- **Textiles & Clothing:** Donate to local second-hand charities, swap parties (Kluba vakari), or specialized textile sorting containers.
- **Electronics & Batteries:** Deliver to municipal e-waste recycling stations (Šķiroto atkritumu pieņemšanas punkti) or electronics retail drop-off points.
- **Books & Media:** Offer to local community libraries, free Little Free Libraries, or community book exchanges.

## The Slot Machine in Your Pocket

Your smartphone is the most sophisticated dopamine-delivery mechanism ever invented. Every pull-to-refresh motion, red notification badge, and algorithm-curated short video feed is engineered to exploit human vulnerability to variable rewards.

By reclaiming control of your phone's interface, you reclaim hours of focused attention every day.

> "If you do not pay for the product, you are the product being sold."

## Tactical Lockdown Protocol

### 1. The Zero-Icon Home Screen
Move all app icons off your primary home screen. Leave it completely empty or with just 1 or 2 essential utility icons (e.g. Phone, Notes). When you need an app, launch it by typing its name in the search bar. This deliberate micro-friction breaks unconscious thumb reflex.

### 2. The Monochromatic (Grayscale) Filter
Vibrant app icons are designed to look like candy. Inverting your screen to black-and-white (via Accessibility Shortcut on iOS and Android) immediately strips the visual allure from social media feeds.

### 3. Eliminate Infinite Feeds
Delete social media apps from your phone. If you need to check Instagram, Twitter/X, or LinkedIn, do so exclusively via a desktop web browser.

### 4. Zero Algorithmic Notifications
Disable 100% of notifications except direct phone calls and direct personal messages from living people.

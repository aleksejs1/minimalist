## The Myth of Multitasking

Neuroscience has proven definitively: the human brain cannot perform multiple high-cognition tasks simultaneously. What we call "multitasking" is actually **rapid task switching**.

Each switch incurs an expensive **Context Switching Tax** (attention residue), leaving your mind depleted and prone to errors.

True productivity is achieved not by doing more things faster, but by doing fewer things with undivided immersion.

> "To produce at your peak level you need to work for extended periods with full concentration on a single task free from distraction." — Cal Newport

## The Rule of 3 Protocol

1. **Morning Selection:** Every morning, write down exactly **three primary tasks** on an index card or plain-text file.
2. **Time-Boxing:** Block a 90-minute uninterrupted deep work sprint for Task #1 before opening email or messaging apps.
3. **Batching Errands:** Group shallow admin tasks (invoices, emails, quick replies) into a single 45-minute afternoon window.

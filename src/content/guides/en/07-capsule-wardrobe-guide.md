## The Closet Paradox: Full Wardrobe, Nothing to Wear

Studies in consumer psychology show a consistent pattern: **we wear 20% of our clothes 80% of the time.** The other 80% consists of ill-fitting items, impulse sale purchases, uncomfortable fabrics, and clothes kept for a hypothetical "someday."

Every morning, scanning an overflowing closet demands cognitive willpower before your day has even begun.

A capsule wardrobe solves this by curating a cohesive collection of high-quality, interchangeable garments.

> "Simplicity is the keynote of all true elegance." — Coco Chanel

## The Core Principles of a Minimalist Wardrobe

### 1. Cohesive Color Palette
Build around 2–3 neutral base colors (e.g. black, navy, charcoal, white, cream) and 1–2 muted accent colors. When every top matches every bottom, getting dressed takes 30 seconds.

### 2. Fabric Quality Over Trend Speed
Choose natural, breathable, and durable materials: heavyweight cotton, merino wool, linen, and raw denim. Quality garments look better, last for years, and require fewer replacements.

### 3. Project 333 (Courtney Carver)
A widely respected challenge: dress with only **33 items for 3 months** (including clothes, accessories, and shoes; excluding workout gear, underwear, and loungewear). Most people discover they feel more stylish with fewer options.

## The 30-Item Seasonal Formula
- **Tops:** 8–10 (t-shirts, shirts, knitwear)
- **Bottoms:** 4–6 (trousers, jeans, shorts/skirts)
- **Outerwear:** 3–4 (jackets, coats)
- **Shoes:** 3–4 pairs (boots, sneakers, dress shoes)
- **Accessories:** 3–4 (belt, scarf, hat)

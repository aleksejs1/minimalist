## Digital Hoarding: The Invisible Burden

Because digital storage costs pennies per gigabyte, most people accumulate hundreds of thousands of redundant screenshots, blurry photos, duplicate downloads, and forgotten desktop files.

Even though digital clutter takes up no physical floor space, it creates persistent cognitive drag whenever you need to find an important document.

> "A complex filing system is a monument to procrastination."

## The PARA Framework for File Sanity

Instead of nesting folders 10 levels deep, organize your entire digital life into four top-level buckets:

1. **Projects:** Active efforts with a specific deadline (e.g. `2026-Tax-Return`, `minimalist-website-launch`).
2. **Areas:** Ongoing spheres of responsibility without an end date (e.g. `Health`, `Finance`, `Legal`).
3. **Resources:** Reference materials, manuals, and templates you might consult later.
4. **Archive:** Inactive items from the previous three categories. Move completed projects here immediately.

## The 3-2-1 Backup Strategy in Simple Plain-Text
- **3 copies** of your vital data (documents, family photos, code).
- **2 different media types** (e.g., local SSD + external encrypted USB drive).
- **1 offsite backup** (e.g., encrypted cloud storage or a drive kept at a family member's house).

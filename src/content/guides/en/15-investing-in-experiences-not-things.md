## The Cornell University Research

Groundbreaking research led by Dr. Thomas Gilovich at Cornell University demonstrated that purchasing experiences generates greater, more enduring happiness than purchasing material goods.

Why?
1. **Experiences become part of our identity:** You are not your sofa, but you are the sum of your journeys, conversations, and learned skills.
2. **Experiences resist comparison:** It is easy to compare a newer smartphone with an older one, but impossible to compare a hike in a pine forest with a dinner with close friends.
3. **Memories take up zero square meters:** They require no cleaning, insurance, or maintenance.

> "The best things in life aren't things."

## What to Invest In Instead
- **Physical health & restorative sleep:** Quality mattress, nutrition, movement.
- **Micro-adventures & nature:** Exploring local coastlines, trails, and cultural events.
- **Skill mastery & learning:** Languages, cooking, writing, practical crafts.

## Visual Friction at Your Desk

Every loose cord, sticky note, stack of unopened mail, and empty mug on your desk competes for neural bandwidth in your peripheral vision.

A clean, uncluttered workspace signals to your nervous system that you are ready for deep, focused work.

> "Order on your desk creates calm in your mind."

## The 5 Rules of an Essentialist Desk

1. **One Input Device & One Output Screen:** Keep only the tools required for the current task.
2. **Invisible Cables:** Use under-desk cable trays, velcro ties, and minimal USB-C hubs to eliminate cord clutter.
3. **The Clean Slate Policy:** At the end of each workday, clear 100% of physical debris off your desk. Start tomorrow morning with a fresh surface.
4. **Ergonomic Fundamentals Over Gadgets:** Invest in a sturdy desk, ergonomic chair, correct monitor height, and warm diffused lighting.

## Dunbar's Number and Social Bandwidth

Anthropologist Robin Dunbar demonstrated that human cognitive capacity limits our close social network to roughly **150 contacts**, and our intimate circle of deep trust to just **5 people**.

Social media gives us the illusion that we can maintain 2,000 "friends." In practice, this results in broad, shallow acquaintances while starving genuine relationships of deep presence.

> "A friend to everyone is a friend to no one." — Aristotle

## The Practice of Relational Minimalism

1. **Undivided Attention:** When dining or conversing with a friend or family member, put your phone completely out of sight. A phone placed face-down on a table still reduces cognitive empathy.
2. **Audit Energy-Draining Ties:** Identify one-sided or drama-heavy relationships that leave you emotionally depleted, and gradually fade interaction without confrontation.
3. **Deepen the Core:** Direct 80% of your relational time and warmth toward the 5 to 10 people who truly matter to you.

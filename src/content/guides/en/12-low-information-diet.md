## The Breaking News Industrial Complex

Modern news media does not exist to inform you; it exists to keep you in a state of heightened arousal so you will refresh the page and view advertisements.

Consuming real-time news alerts does not make you wiser; it floods your nervous system with cortisol while providing almost zero actionable knowledge.

> "To be well informed, you need to read slowly and selectively." — Rolf Dobelli

## The Three Pillars of an Information Diet

### 1. The 30-Day News Fast
Stop reading daily news feeds and sensationalist opinion columns for 30 days. If an event is truly momentous (a natural disaster or major law change), people in your real life will tell you.

### 2. High-Density Content Over Real-Time Snippets
Replace 50 superficial tweets or short videos with 1 deeply researched book chapter or long-form essay. High-density ideas have a half-life measured in decades, not minutes.

### 3. Asynchronous Pull Over Push
Never allow algorithms to push content to you. Use an open-source RSS reader (like NetNewsWire or Feeder) to pull updates on your own schedule from trusted independent authors.

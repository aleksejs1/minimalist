## Calendar Debt: The Modern Malady

Just as your home fills with unused physical objects, your calendar naturally accumulates non-essential meetings, polite social obligations, and projects you agreed to out of temporary discomfort saying "No."

When your calendar has zero margin, your nervous system remains in chronic low-grade fight-or-flight mode.

> "If you don't prioritize your life, someone else will." — Greg McKeown

## How to Say No Graciously and Firmly

1. **The Positive No:** Anchor your refusal in a genuine commitment (*"I cannot take on this new project because I am fully dedicated to finishing [X] by next week."*).
2. **The 24-Hour Delay:** Never say "Yes" on the spot. Reply: *"Let me check my priorities and get back to you tomorrow."*
3. **Protecting Buffer Blocks:** Schedule recurring 2-hour "White Space" blocks in your calendar that cannot be booked by anyone else.

## A Quiet Corner on the Web

The modern web often feels exhausting. Almost every website you visit greets you with three cookie consent popups, asks for notification permissions, demands your email address, and auto-plays video advertisements while dozens of tracking scripts record your mouse movements.

**minimalist.lv was created to be the exact opposite.**

This project is an intentional, quiet refuge on the internet — dedicated to exploring simplicity in our physical spaces, digital lives, personal finances, and everyday tools.

> "Perfection is achieved, not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away." — Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

## What You Will Find Here

The platform is structured into three clear pillars:

### 1. The Core Knowledge Base (Guides)
A structured curriculum of in-depth guides covering the foundations of intentional living — from decluttering methodologies and capsule wardrobes to digital hygiene and financial independence.

### 2. The Weekly 5-Minute Blog
Every week, we publish a concise, 5-minute essay exploring practical micro-habits, seasonal reflections, and actionable experiments designed to help you declutter your life one small step at a time.

### 3. The Minimalist Tool Directory
Inspired by ethical platforms like *Privacy Guides*, our software directory curates lightweight, distraction-free, local-first, and open-source software. We test and recommend tools that respect your attention, protect your privacy, and operate without bloat.

## Our Technical Commitments

To practice what we preach, **minimalist.lv** is built with radical minimalism:

- **100% Static & Zero Client Tracking:** No Google Analytics, no Facebook pixels, no advertising beacons.
- **Lightning Fast:** Pure static HTML and CSS rendered at the edge for instant loading times worldwide.
- **Multilingual from Day One:** Full content parity in English, Russian, and Latvian.
- **Open Source:** Content-as-code hosted transparently on GitHub.

Thank you for being here. We invite you to explore the guides, brew a cup of tea, and take a deep breath.
